; Rail Runner obstacle patterns, hand-designed.
;
; Each pattern starts with "= name tier weight". Then one row per 1 u of track (about 4 m),
; three lanes per row (left middle right). The TOP row is the FAR end: read it like the track
; ahead of you. Glyphs:
;   .  empty                         T  parked train (a vertical run of T is one train)
;   /  ramp up onto the train above  O  oncoming train: front, where it meets you
;   o  oncoming train body (above O) _  low barrier: jump over it
;   ^  high barrier: roll under it   #  wall barrier: change lanes
; Tier = first speed band the pattern may appear in (0 = from the start, 3 = fastest).
; Weight = relative pick frequency. test_escape.py proves every pattern can be survived from
; every lane at every speed it can appear at, and ends with the player back on the ground.
