## Data and models used to train the networks

| Network | Trained on | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pixel** (`llm/`) | chats generated with [Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct) (Apache-2.0) | the weights (`llm/train/checkpoints/pixel_v2.pxm`) are included |
| **Doodle** "Finish it" (`paint/`) | Google's [*Quick, Draw!* dataset](https://github.com/googlecreativelab/quickdraw-dataset), CC BY 4.0 | a sketch-rnn-style decoder (Ha & Eck, 2017); the weights are included. *Quick, Draw!* data © Google, CC BY 4.0 |
| **Dream** (`dream/`) | clip art generated with FLUX 3 Image Klein (Black Forest Labs), FLUX Kommunity License | **the weights and the generated pictures are not included** (see [`dream/README.md`](dream/README.md) and [`dream/LICENSE-FLUX.md`](dream/LICENSE-FLUX.md)). Dream's subject list is taken from the *Quick, Draw!* categories |
| **Guess My Doodle** (`guess/`) | Google's [*Quick, Draw!* dataset](https://github.com/googlecreativelab/quickdraw-dataset), CC BY 4.0 (150 categories) | the weights (`guess/app/data/Guess.gmdl`) are included. *Quick, Draw!* data © Google, CC BY 4.0, credited in the app's About box |
| **Scribble Calc** (`calc/`) | [UJI Pen Characters v2](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/177) (Prat, Castro, Llorens, Marzal, Vilar) and [Pen-Based Recognition of Handwritten Digits](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/81) (Alpaydin, Alimoglu), UCI Machine Learning Repository, both CC BY 4.0; plus synthetic strokes | the weights (`calc/model/scribble_model.npz`) are included. The datasets were modified (resampled, slanted, drawn as thick 1-bit strokes); credited in the app's About box |
| **Tune** (`tune/`) | ABC transcriptions of three public-domain tune books: O'Neill's *Music of Ireland* (1903, transcribed by the O'Neill's Project), Ryan's *Mammoth Collection* (1883, the Ryan/Cole project) and Kerr's *Merry Melodies* (c. 1875 to 1890, John Chambers), from John Chambers' collection at trillian.mit.edu | only the notes, lengths and bar lines are used. The weights (`tune/model/`) are included; the transcribers are credited in the app's About box |
| **Tank Duel** (`tanks/`) | no outside data: every training game is played in its own simulation (reinforcement learning against scripted opponents and by self-play) | the policies (`tanks/policies/`) are included |

The networks were trained on the Digital Research Alliance of Canada's clusters.

## Tools

The Mac programs are built with [Retro68](https://github.com/autc04/Retro68) (GCC for classic 68k
Macs) and tested in [MAME](https://www.mamedev.org/). Disk images are made with
[hfsutils](https://www.mars.org/home/rob/proj/hfs/).
