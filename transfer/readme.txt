Narrow Screen 1.0

This disk carries the Narrow Screen file for the Macintosh Plus.

1. Copy "Narrow Screen" into the System Folder of the Plus's startup disk.
2. Restart the Plus.
3. Open Apple menu > Control Panel > Narrow Screen.
4. Move the Left and Right sliders until the two guide lines sit just
   inside the visible part of the screen, then click Restart.

Left moves in 16-pixel steps; Right moves 1 pixel at a time.
Hold Shift while the Plus starts up to skip Narrow Screen for one boot.
Set both sliders to 0 to turn it off.
