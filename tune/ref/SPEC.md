# Tune integer inference: specification

`tuneref.py` is normative; the Mac (`tune/app/engine.c`, `grammar.c`, the kernels generated by
`tune/app/gen_kernels.py`) computes the same integers. `test_host.py` (C engine on the host) and
`tune/app/bench/check_tokens.py` (the 68000 in MAME) check that tunes come out token for token.

## Tokens and grammar (`tune/corpus/vocab.py`)

220 tokens: PAD, BOS, EOS; bar lines `|` `||` `|:` `:|` `::` `|1` `:|2`; UP / DOWN (raise or lower
the next note a semitone from the mode's scale); T3 (the next three notes are a triplet); 7 pickup
lengths; 8 rests and 24 × 8 notes (diatonic degree −5..18 from the tonic × nominal length 3, 6, 9,
12, 18, 24, 30, 36 ticks; an eighth note is 6 ticks). Tunes are key-free: the tonic is degree 0 and
the app transposes to the chosen key.

`State` tracks the bar position (ticks), triplets, the bar index in the section (−1 for a pickup
bar), the section index, first/second endings and a history of the notes that started at each
bar position (per section and bar). `State.allowed(maxtok, maxbars)` is the grammar: notes must fit
the bar, plain bar lines close full bars, section lines (`||` `|:` `:|` `::` `:|2`) may also end a
short bar (the next notes are the following section's pickup), `|1` only at a full bar, `:|2` only
after `|1`, EOS anywhere a bar is open or after a section line; past `maxbars` only notes that
finish the bar, then EOS or `:|`.

## Network

Two GRU layers of 160 units. Layer 1's gate inputs are a sum of 11 rows of 480 int16 (Q9: 1/512)
taken from tables: the previous token's symbol and duration, the bar position, triplet state, bar
and section index, the look-back inputs (symbol and duration of the note that started at this bar
position 4 bars earlier in this section, and at this bar of the previous section), and one row for
the tune (type + meter + mode, clamped once per tune). Every table entry is clamped to
±2978 = 32767 / 11, so the sum never overflows 16 bits.

Weights: ternary (BitNet b1.58 absmean per row), packed 5 to a 16-bit code; per row a multiplier
M ≤ 32767 and per matrix a shift k with `scale(acc) = wrap16(((acc · M) >> 16) << k)` (the high
word of `muls.w`, then `asl.w`).

Per token, per layer (H = 160, 3H rows interleaved r, z, n per unit on the Mac):

1. Layer 2's input: `x = sat16(scale_ih(W_ih · hq1) + b_ih)`.
2. `acc = W_hh · hq` (exact: |acc| ≤ 160 · 127).
3. `r = SIG[(sat16(x_r + scale(acc_r)) >> 3) + 4096]`, `z` likewise (Q15).
4. `v = sat16(scale(acc_n) + bhn)`, `n = TANH[(sat16(x_n + ((r · v) >> 15)) >> 3) + 4096]`
   (h units: 16256 = 1.0).
5. `h = n + ((z · (h − n)) >> 15)`, `hq = (h + 64) >> 7` (−127..127).

SIG and TANH have 8192 entries over the whole int16 range in steps of 8 (1/64).
Output: `L = sat16(scale_out(W_out · hq2) + B_out)` (Q8 nats), computed only for allowed tokens.

## Sampling

One allowed token: taken, no random number. Otherwise `Lt = sat16((L · invT) >> 8)` (invT 256 =
temperature 1), `p = EXP[(Lmax − Lt) >> 3]` (Q8, EXP[k] = 256·e^(−k/32)) when `Lmax − Lt ≤ cut` (in 1/256
nat; the app uses 1152 = 4.5 nats, so tokens less than 1.1 % as likely as the best are never
picked), else 0; `P = Σp`;
`rng = rng · 1103515245 + 12345 (mod 2^32)`, `u = rng >> 16`, `r = (u · P) >> 16`; the token is
the first whose running sum of p exceeds r. The seed is the initial rng.

## Model file

`export.py` writes `model.npz` (for `tuneref.py`) and `model.bin` (big-endian, read by
`engine.c tm_load`; the app carries it as resource 'TMDL' 128). Layout in `export.write_bin`.
